## Decision and scope

The source invocation cohort is the undirected connected component of uniquely
resolved literal calls. It includes callers and connected siblings. It does not
include workflows merely because they share data. The saved package investigation
records 32 members and 31 call sites; MASTER_PREP has 19 outward nowait launches.
Those historical counts are not a fresh production observation. This source
inventory is not an approved architecture or execution DAG. Missing, dynamic and
ambiguous calls remain unverified boundaries.
